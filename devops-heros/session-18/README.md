## Student Task

For each service, document:

1. What the service does and which problem it solves.
2. Its main concepts and components.
3. At least two practical use cases.
4. One security, availability, or cost consideration.
5. A short comparison with a related service where appropriate.
6. A reflection describing what you learned and what you would like to practice next.

Do not include passwords, access keys, or other secrets in these files.
